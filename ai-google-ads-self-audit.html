FAQ

What does this tool check in a self audit? ⌄
It reviews core performance blockers: conversion tracking quality, wasted spend patterns, campaign structure basics, search term intent match, ad relevance, and landing page friction.
What are “critical” vs “warning” issues? ⌄
Critical issues can break measurement or burn budget fast (missing tracking, wrong conversion, extreme mismatch). Warning issues reduce efficiency (weak ad assets, overlap, poor segmentation) but are usually easier to optimize.
Should I run this audit monthly? ⌄
Yes—monthly is a strong baseline. Run it sooner if you change budgets, launch new campaigns, switch bidding strategies, or update your landing pages.
