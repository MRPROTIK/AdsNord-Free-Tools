FAQ

What is “landing page friction”? ⌄
Friction is anything that slows down or confuses users—unclear offer, weak proof, too many steps, poor mobile layout, slow loading, or a CTA that isn’t obvious.
What should I fix first to improve conversions quickly? ⌄
Start with above-the-fold clarity: a clear promise, who it’s for, one primary CTA, and 1–2 proof points (reviews, results, guarantees). Then reduce distractions and make the next step easy on mobile.
Will this also help reduce Google Ads wasted spend? ⌄
Yes. Better intent match and stronger conversion flow means more conversions from the same clicks—so you waste less budget on users who bounce.
