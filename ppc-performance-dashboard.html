FAQ

What time range should I analyze first? ⌄
Start with the last 14 or 30 days, then compare to the previous period. That makes spikes in CPA, drops in CVR, and budget waste easier to spot.
What does “wasted spend” mean in this dashboard? ⌄
It usually means spend that isn’t producing meaningful conversions—often caused by low-intent queries, poor landing page fit, weak offers, or bidding pushing into expensive auctions.
Will this replace a full Google Ads audit? ⌄
No—this is a fast performance snapshot. A full audit also checks tracking accuracy, campaign structure, search terms, ad assets, and landing page behavior.
