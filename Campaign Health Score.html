FAQ

What is a “Campaign Health Score”? ⌄
It’s a simple score that summarizes how ready your campaign is to perform efficiently—based on tracking, structure, intent alignment, ad assets, and landing page fit.
What counts as “critical” issues? ⌄
Critical issues are problems that can break measurement or burn budget fast—like missing/incorrect conversion tracking, overly broad targeting, or a major mismatch between ads and landing page intent.
How often should I check campaign health? ⌄
Monthly is a good baseline. Re-check after big changes like new offers, budget increases, switching bidding strategies, or landing page updates.
