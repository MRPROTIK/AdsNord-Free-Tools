FAQ

Is this AI ad copy generator free? ⌄
Yes. This is a free tool to help you generate ad copy ideas faster. Always review final copy for accuracy, claims, and policy compliance before publishing.
How do I make the copy convert better (not just get clicks)? ⌄
Use qualifiers (who it’s for), proof (reviews, delivery time, warranty), and a single clear next step. Then ensure the landing page answers the promise in the ad above the fold.
Can I use the output for Responsive Search Ads (RSA)? ⌄
Yes. Use the variants as your starting asset set, then diversify angles (benefit, proof, urgency, location) and avoid repeating the same meaning across many headlines.
